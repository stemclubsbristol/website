Workshop Terms, Safety & Operating Agreement

To ensure safe, high-impact, and smooth-running sessions, STEM Clubs Bristol operates under the following straightforward agreements for all on-site school workshops and STEM clubs:

1. Venue & Infrastructure Requirements

Computer Room Access: The host school agrees to provide access to a suitable computer suite or ICT room equipped with student workstations.

Connectivity & Ports: Workstations must provide functional USB port access (for flashing microcontrollers) and reliable internet access (for web-based programming environments like MakeCode and the Arduino Cloud Editor).

2. Hardware Handling & Supervision

Kit Care: All microcontrollers (micro:bits, Raspberry Pi Picos, Arduino Unos), sensors, and robotic buggies remain the property of STEM Clubs Bristol and are loaned for the duration of the session.

Supervision: Host school staff or supervising teachers must remain present in the room during the workshop to assist with student behaviour management and pastoral care.

Damage/Loss: While normal wear and tear is expected during hands-on building, the host school or institution may be invoiced at cost for any unreturned items or severe damage to hardware components.

3. Health, Safety & Electrical Compliance

All electrical hardware, multi-blocks, and sensors brought on-site by STEM Clubs Bristol are low-voltage DC powered or maintained in safe working order.

Supervising staff and students agree to follow basic workshop safety rules (e.g., keeping drinks away from open circuits, reporting damaged jumper wires or loose leads immediately).

4. Safeguarding & Medical Responsibility

STEM Clubs Bristol session leaders operate in full compliance with UK safeguarding standards, hold appropriate enhanced DBS certification, and will always adhere to the host school's on-site safeguarding and fire evacuation procedures.

First Aid & Medical Needs: The host school retains primary responsibility for student first aid, welfare, and medical needs (including allergies, asthma, and emergency medication such as EpiPens) for all participating students throughout the session.
