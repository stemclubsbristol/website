About STEM Clubs Bristol

Led by an experienced secondary Computer Science educator and school leader with an extensive background in physical computing, STEM Clubs Bristol bridges the gap between abstract theory and real-world engineering.

We provide all the hardware and resources—allowing your students to move beyond the screen and get hands-on with micro:controllers, robotics, and physical code.

School Offerings & Formats

Single & Half-Day Workshops

Immersive, high-energy sessions (such as our popular Up and Running with micro:bit V2 workshop) tailored to kickstart a curriculum unit, run as an enrichment day, or spark interest during STEM week.

Class Size: Strictly Capped at 15

Sequential Lesson Series

A structured multi-week program delivered directly at your school, building core physical computing, debugging, and problem-solving skills progressively.

Bespoke Sessions

Fully customizable workshops designed to match your specific key stage requirements, project goals, or cross-curricular themes.

Computer Science INSET & Staff Training

Empower your teaching team with practical, confidence-building professional development. We help non-specialist and specialist teachers alike get comfortable using physical hardware like micro:bits, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi directly in the classroom.
